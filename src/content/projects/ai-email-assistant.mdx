## Problem

Inbox work is replies, tasks, and search, not one chat box. A useful assistant has to split those jobs and keep them off the request thread.

## Approach

A React and FastAPI app with Redis and Celery for background work. The public features are smart replies, task extraction, and natural-language inbox queries.

## Results

The public claim is that feature set. Latency or quality numbers are not published on this page.
