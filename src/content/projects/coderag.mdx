## Problem

Asking questions over a codebase needs an index, not a full-repo prompt. Functions and classes have to be retrieved before an LLM can answer from them.

## Approach

A Python indexer that extracts functions and classes, embeds them with UniXcoder, stores FAISS indices, and answers questions with an LLM over the retrieved context.

## Results

The public claim is the retrieve-then-read loop: embeddings, FAISS, and LLM Q&A. Retrieval accuracy numbers are not published on this page.
