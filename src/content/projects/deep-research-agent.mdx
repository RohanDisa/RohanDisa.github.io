## Problem

Research work splits across planning, gathering, and writing. A single linear chat does not keep those jobs separate or run gathering in parallel.

## Approach

A LangGraph agent that splits the work: multi-agent planning, parallel research, and synthesis of findings.

## Results

The public claim is the architecture: planning, parallel research, and synthesis. Numbers beyond that are not published on this page.
